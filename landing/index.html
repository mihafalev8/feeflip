◆ Mechanics

How The Lottery Works

A fully automated, provably fair lottery powered by PumpFun creator fees. No trust required.

01
💸

Fees Collected

Every buy and sell generates creator fees. The bot claims them automatically via PumpPortal Lightning API.

02
🏦

Pool Grows

Claimed fees accumulate in the jackpot pool. More trading volume means a bigger prize for the next drawing.

03
🎲

Drawing Happens

At configurable intervals, a weighted random drawing selects a winner. Token balance determines probability.

04
🏆

Winner Gets SOL

The entire jackpot is sent directly to the winner's wallet in SOL. Announced instantly on Telegram.
